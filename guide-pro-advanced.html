Open Options

Click the extension icon, then Options at the bottom-left of the popup. Everything below lives on these tabs.

General

  • Default group / result mode: what the hotkey and right-click use, and whether results are tabs or one merged tab.
  • Consolidated options: how merged results look (cards or list), are grouped and sorted, and how many results are kept per engine (Pro: no cap).
  • Tabs: open in the background, next to the current tab, and in a browser tab group.
  • Search bar on selection: optional, asks for access to all sites.
  • Remember my searches: on by default, kept on this device only; choose 50–500 or clear it.

A group’s own mode (Groups tab) overrides the default here; a choice made in the popup for a single search overrides both.

Engines and the custom engine editor

Press + Add engine to make a new card, then fill in:

NameHow it appears on buttons and chips.
KeywordA short word for the address bar: hubp + Space + keyword + Space + search.
URLThe site’s search address with {q} where your search goes (%s and {searchTerms} also work). For addresses like /tag/red-shoes/ use {slug}. Tip: search for “test” on the site, copy the address, then swap “test” for {q}.
Open in its own tab onlyNever merge this site into the combined page; just open it.
Parsers (expandable)Optional rules that let the hub read the site’s results so they can be merged. Skip unless you know the site’s layout.
Sign-inOptional. Most sites you are already signed in to just work. For a site that asks you to sign in (X, for example), first create a master password at the top of the Engines tab; you must do this before a login can be saved. Saved logins are encrypted with it. They stay unlocked until you close the browser, so after a restart unlock them again at the top of the Engines tab, or nothing will be filled in. Then enter the site’s username and password under Sign-in and press Save sign-in and allow site access.

You can also turn any built-in site on or off, rename it, change its keyword and press Test to check it.

Groups

A group is a named set of engines. Tick the engines for each group, give it its own result mode, and choose which type it searches (Web, Images, Videos). The popup shows your groups as big buttons. You can make your own groups and give them keywords.

History

Your full list of searches. Search again reruns one, Delete removes one, the filter box narrows the list. Nothing is synced or exported, and InPrivate searches are never recorded.

Import / Export

  • Download settings (JSON): a backup of your engines, groups and options.
  • Restore from JSON: choose a backup file to bring it all back, e.g. on another computer.
  • Quick-add engines: one per line, Name | https://site/search?q=%s | optional keyword, then Add these engines.
  • Restore app defaults (orange): resets everything. Download a backup first.

Your Pro license

  • Buy Pro once, then paste the license key from the purchase email into Options → Pro and press Activate.
  • The key is re-checked about once a week and keeps working offline for up to 30 days. Only the key is sent, to Lemon Squeezy; no searches.
  • If the key is refunded or disabled, Pro features lock again, but nothing you saved is deleted.
  • Moving to another computer, or about to uninstall? Press Deactivate License on This Computer first, then activate it on the new one. Removing the extension without doing this keeps one of your activations in use.

Testing and troubleshooting

The hub can read some sites’ results so they can be merged into one page. Sites change, so use Test all engines now after installing, and whenever a site stops showing in merged results.

How to test

  1. Open Options → Engines and press Grant access to custom engine sites… once. Accept the browser’s prompt. Without it, sites outside the built-in list fail with “Failed to fetch (site access may be missing)”. Pressing it again after you add engines is harmless.
  2. Press Test all engines now, in the same row of buttons at the top of the Engines tab. After a few seconds the page scrolls down to a report with your browser and extension version, and one line per site and type. Press Clear test results beside it to empty the list when you have finished. A button you press keeps a blue border for 5 seconds so you can see it registered.
  3. Read the first symbol on each line: ✓ results were read and can be merged; ! the page loaded but the hub could not read results from it; ✕ the site failed or is open-in-tab only.
  4. For one site only, press Test with “open source” on that site’s card.

What the messages mean

Failed to fetch (site access may be missing)Do step 1 above, then test again. If it still fails, the site may be down or blocked on your network.
Opened in its own tab onlyNot an error. That engine is set to open in its own tab (Google is, by design).
Blocked or CAPTCHA page, HTTP 403 or 429The site refused an automatic request or is rate limiting you. Wait a few minutes and test again, open the site in a normal tab to solve any check, or leave it as open-in-tab.
Timed outThe site was too slow or is waiting for a sign-in. Try again later, or sign in on the site in a normal tab.
Sign-in required (HTTP 401)Add a username and password under Sign-in on the engine’s card, or sign in on the site in a normal tab.
Needs a free API keyPixabay is one. Get the free key on the site and enter it on the engine’s card.
HTTP 404The search address has changed. Search on the site in a normal tab and copy the new address into the card’s URL box, with {q} where the search words go.
HTTP 200 but no results could be read (!)The site’s layout changed, or it showed a consent, login or check page. Open the site in a normal tab and accept anything it asks, then test again.
⚠ beside a site nameThe site blocks automatic reading (Openverse), so it always opens in its own tab and the test shows “Opened in its own tab only”. If it shows a check or checkbox, clear it once in the tab that opens. Not an error.
🔑 beside a site nameThe site needs sign-in details or a free key (X, Pixabay). Add them under Sign-in on the engine’s card in Options → Engines.
Still not working? Email Support at multisearchhub@gmail.com with a screenshot of the full Test all engines now report (or paste its text), plus the name of the site that fails and what you searched for. We will investigate the issue and advise further. A site that shows ! or ✕ still opens in its own tab, so a search is never lost.

Updating or moving the folder

  • Update: unzip the new version over the same folder, then press Reload on the extension’s card in edge://extensions.
  • Move the folder: Edge remembers the folder you first loaded, so Reload cannot follow a folder you have moved or renamed. The easiest way is to leave the folder where it is and unzip each new version over it. To use a new location, first download your settings (Options → Import / Export → Download settings (JSON)), then press Remove on the extension’s card, press Load unpacked and pick the new folder, then use Restore from JSON. Removing the extension deletes its saved settings, sign-ins and license activation, and sign-ins are not part of the backup, so you will enter them again (Pro may ask for your license key again, which uses another activation).
