Multi Search Hub Pro

Search many sites at once · quick guide · Advanced guide

Install in 3 steps

  1. Unzip the download into a folder you will keep, e.g. C:\Zip\Multi Search Hub Pro. Don’t delete it later; the browser reads the extension from there.
  2. In Edge open edge://extensions (Chrome: chrome://extensions). Turn Developer mode on, click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  3. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Multi Search Hub Pro. Open Options → Pro, paste the license key from your purchase email and press Activate.

Using it

Click the icon, type a search, choose a group, press Search. Results open in tabs or in one merged page. That’s all you need; the numbers on the picture explain each part.

Updating later

Unzip the new version over the same folder and press Reload on the extension’s card in edge://extensions. Your settings are kept. If you installed from the Chrome or Edge store, updates arrive automatically.

Pro is a separate extension from the free Multi Search Hub. They can be installed side by side (different icon, address-bar keyword and shortcuts). Without a valid key this extension works with the free limits.

Handy tips

From the address barType hubp, press Space or Tab, then your search.
From any pageSelect text, right-click and choose the Multi Search Hub Pro search. Or press Alt+Shift+R.
Sites marked “!”A few sites (such as Google Images) can’t be merged, so they open in their own tab.
Your searchesRecent searches are remembered on this device only. Clear them any time (Clear all, or Options → History) or switch it off in Options → General.
