Meet Multi Search Hub: one starting point for a broader search. Open the search window and enter your topic. Pick a built-in group, then choose the sources you want to include. Search the web, images, or videos without retyping your question on every site. Compatible sources can appear in a combined results view. Other sources open in their own tabs, so you can explore them directly. Switch between list and card layouts, and choose the theme that works for you. The free edition includes built-in groups and up to fifteen results per engine, with sixty results total in the combined view. Pro adds custom engines and groups, saved presets, search history, backup and restore, and encrypted saved sign-ins. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Start free in Chrome or Edge, then upgrade if those extra tools fit the way you work. Visit multi search hub dot github dot io for store links, feature comparisons, and the user guides. Search once.
