Here is how to get started with Multi Search Hub. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons, using the links on our official website. Open the search window and type your topic. Choose a built-in group, then select the engines you want to include. Search web pages, images, or videos from one starting point. Compatible sources appear in a combined results view. Other services open in their own tabs. Not every provider can be combined, and available results can vary. Try the list and card layouts to see which suits your research. Open useful results at their original source, and check their accuracy and usage rights. The free edition includes built-in groups, source selection, themes, and core search. Combined results are capped at fifteen per engine and sixty overall. Pro adds tools for repeat research: custom engines and groups, named presets, multi-group search, and search history. Backup and restore help preserve your settings. Pro also includes optional encrypted saved sign-ins. Follow the guide for your master password and backups, and never share a license key publicly. Pro offers higher configurable result limits, but the number available still depends on each provider. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Check the current price and activation instructions on the website. For your first search, pick a topic you know and compare a few sources. Then try another built-in group. If you keep choosing the same sources, explore Pro's presets and custom groups. Find the feature comparison, store links, and step-by-step guides at multi search hub dot github dot io. Search once.
