Multi Search Hub Pro · Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026. Contact: multisearchhub@gmail.com

Summary

Multi Search Hub Pro does not collect, sell, share or receive any of your data. The developer has no server for the extension and sees nothing you do with it. (The Pro license key check goes to Lemon Squeezy; see below.)

What is stored, and where

The following is stored only in your own browser profile on your device (browser local storage). It is never uploaded to the developer or synced to other devices:

  • Settings: your engines, groups, theme and options.
  • Search history: your recent search words, the type and group used, and the time. It can be switched off in Options, can be cleared at any time, and is not recorded for private (InPrivate or Incognito) windows. It is not included in settings backups. Without a valid Pro license, no history is kept.
  • Pro license: your license key and the date it was last checked.
  • Site sign-ins (optional): if you choose to enter a username and password for a site, they are encrypted with a master password you choose, kept on your device only, never included in backups, and are used only for that site. You can delete them in Options.

Uninstalling the extension removes all of this.

What is sent to other websites

When you run a search, your search words are sent to the search sites you selected (for example Bing, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, YouTube or any custom site you added) so they can return results. This is the same as typing the words on that site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and your use of them is governed by those policies. The extension adds no identifiers and sends nothing to any other party.

Pro license check

When you enter the license key you bought from the developer’s store on Lemon Squeezy, the extension sends that key (and a label such as “Multi Search Hub Pro 1.30.0”) to Lemon Squeezy’s license service to activate it, and again about once a week to confirm it is still valid. No search words, history or other data are sent. Lemon Squeezy handles payment and receives your purchase details under its own privacy policy; the developer does not receive your card details.

Permissions

The extension asks for browser permissions only to provide its features (reading text you select, opening tabs, storing settings, reading the results of the sites you chose). Access to all websites is optional and requested only if you turn on a feature that needs it. Details are on the store listing.

What is not done

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or profiling.
  • No sale or transfer of data to third parties, other than sending your search words to the sites you chose as described above and the license key to Lemon Squeezy.
  • No remote code: all code is inside the extension package.

Children

The extension is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
