Multi Search Hub Pro

The Pro edition of Multi Search Hub. It removes the limits of the free extension and adds history, your own sites, and backup.

Pro coming soonSee what is included

Pro is coming soon. It will be a one-time payment with no subscription, and you will receive a license key by email. Pro will be a separate extension from the free Multi Search Hub, available from the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. The free extension is available now.

What Pro adds

No result limitsThe free extension shows up to 10 results per site and 50 in all. Pro shows everything each site returns.
Search historyA Recent list in the popup, a History tab, and address-bar suggestions. Stored only on your computer.
Your own sitesAdd any site that has a search address, including quick-add lines.
Backup and restoreSave all your settings to a file and restore them anywhere.
