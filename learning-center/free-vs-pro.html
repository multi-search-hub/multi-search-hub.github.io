Multi Search Hub Free vs. Pro: Which Version Do You Need?

A transparent feature comparison and purchase decision guide.

Install Multi Search Hub Free

What Free includes

The Free edition lets you search multiple supported sources, use built-in groups and compare supported consolidated results without buying anything. Its current Chrome listing describes limits of 15 results per engine and 60 results total.

What Pro adds

The Chrome Web Store currently lists Pro as a one-time $9.99 purchase with higher configurable result limits, search history, custom search engines, named presets, Multi-Group Search, settings backup/restore and more personalization. Check the live extension listing before purchase because features or pricing can change.

Who should choose which?

Choose Free if you search several engines occasionally and primarily want a simpler way to compare results. Consider Pro if you routinely repeat complex searches and benefit from presets, history or increased result limits.

Privacy and separate services

Search settings are stored locally in the browser according to the listing. Search terms are sent to selected providers, and some websites require their own login or subscription. Multi Search Hub is independent of those providers.
